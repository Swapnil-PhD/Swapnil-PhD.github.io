Current Project
Macro Signals as a Systematic Trading Overlay
In Progress

Building on the working paper "On the Macroeconomic Foundations of the Anomaly Zoo" (O'Doherty, Wang, Yan), which estimates risk premia for 190 macro factors from FRED-QD and shows they explain CAPM anomalies as well as or better than standard benchmarks. This project pulls the same macro series, builds simplified regression-based mimicking portfolios against tradable factors (market, SMB, HML), and reframes the result as a macro-timing overlay, backtested with real portfolio construction, transaction costs, and risk-adjusted performance metrics (Sharpe, drawdown, turnover).
